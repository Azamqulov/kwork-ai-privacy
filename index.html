Privacy Policy for Kwork AI Tahlil

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

Kwork AI Tahlil is a Chrome extension designed to assist users in analyzing tasks published on Kwork.ru using artificial intelligence technologies.

Information We Collect

The extension may access and process:

  • Content from Kwork task pages opened by the user.
  • User preferences and settings stored locally.
  • Information necessary to generate AI-powered analyses and recommendations.

How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

  • Analyze Kwork task descriptions.
  • Generate recommendations and proposal suggestions.
  • Provide budget and project assessments.
  • Improve extension functionality.

Data Storage

User settings may be stored locally using Chrome Storage APIs. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

The extension may use third-party AI services to process task descriptions and generate analyses. Only the information necessary to fulfill the requested functionality is transmitted.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect processed information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

User Control

Users can stop all data processing at any time by disabling or uninstalling the extension.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated revision date.
